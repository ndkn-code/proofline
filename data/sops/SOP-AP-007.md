# Route a non-PO invoice for approval

## Purpose

Use this SOP to send an invoice that has no purchase order into the approval workflow.

## Steps

1. Open the non-PO invoice from Payables > Invoices > Manage Invoices.
2. Confirm the cost center on each distribution line.
3. Select Actions > Initiate Approval.
4. The invoice routes to the cost center manager first.

## Notes

- Non-PO invoices over $5,000.00 also require a second approver from Finance.
