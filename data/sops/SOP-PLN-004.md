# Lock a planning cycle

## Purpose

Use this SOP to lock a planning cycle after the submission deadline.

## Steps

1. Go to Planning > Cycles > Manage Cycles.
2. Select the cycle and review any open submissions.
3. Select Lock Cycle. Users can no longer edit budget versions in the cycle.

## Notes

- The planning cycle locks at 17:00 Central on business day 3 after the submission deadline.
