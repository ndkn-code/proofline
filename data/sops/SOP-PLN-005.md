# Request a late budget change

## Purpose

Use this SOP to ask for a change to a budget after you have submitted it.

## Steps

1. Go to Planning > Budgets > Change Requests.
2. Select New Request and choose the budget version and department.
3. Describe the change and the amount.
4. Select Submit. The FP&A Systems Analyst reviews late changes.

## Notes

- The planning cycle locks at 17:00 Central on business day 5 after the submission deadline.
