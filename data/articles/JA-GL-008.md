# Reopen a closed period

## Purpose

Use this job aid to request that a closed accounting period be reopened for a correction.

## Steps

1. Submit a Period Reopen Request in the Finance Systems service portal.
2. Include the period, the ledger and the business reason.
3. The GL Accounting Lead reviews the request within 2 business days.
4. After approval, the Period Close Manager changes the period status to Open.
5. Close the period again after the correcting entries post.
