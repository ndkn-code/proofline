# Submit a travel expense report

## Purpose

Use this job aid to claim reimbursement for business travel expenses.

## Steps

1. Go to Expenses > Reports > Create Report.
2. Enter a report purpose and add each expense item with its date, type and amount.
3. Attach an itemized receipt for every expense of $75.00 or more.
4. Select the cost center and project, if any.
5. Select Submit. The report routes to your manager for approval.

## Notes

- Submit expenses within 60 days of the expense date.
- Reimbursements are paid by direct deposit in the next weekly payment run.
