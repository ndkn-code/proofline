# Match an invoice to a purchase order

## Purpose

Use this job aid to match a supplier invoice to purchase order lines and receipts.

## Steps

1. Open the invoice from Payables > Invoices > Manage Invoices.
2. Select Match and choose the purchase order lines to match.
3. Confirm the match level. Goods lines use three-way match against the receipt.
4. Review the quantity and price variances shown on the match page.
5. Select Apply, then select Validate.

## Notes

- Price variance tolerance is 5% or $250.00, whichever is lower.
- Lines without a receipt place the invoice on an RCV hold.
