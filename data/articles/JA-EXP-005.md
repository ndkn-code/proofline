# Claim business mileage

## Purpose

Use this job aid to claim mileage for driving your own car on company business.

## Steps

1. Add an expense item with the type Mileage.
2. Enter the start and end locations and the business miles driven.
3. The rate of $0.70 per mile is applied automatically.
4. Attach a route map and submit the report.
