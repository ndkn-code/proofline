# Reconcile corporate card transactions

## Purpose

Use this job aid to account for every charge on your corporate card statement.

## Steps

1. Go to Expenses > Corporate Card > Unreconciled Transactions.
2. Select each transaction and add it to an expense report.
3. Enter the business purpose and attach the receipt.
4. Mark personal charges as Personal. They are deducted from your next reimbursement.
5. Submit the report. Card transactions must be reconciled within 30 days of the statement date.
