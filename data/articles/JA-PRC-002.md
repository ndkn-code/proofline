# Convert an approved requisition to a purchase order

## Purpose

Use this job aid to turn approved requisition lines into a purchase order.

## Steps

1. Go to Purchasing > Requisitions > Process Requisitions.
2. Filter by the status Approved and select the requisition lines.
3. Select Add to Document Builder and choose the supplier and supplier site.
4. Select Create to generate the purchase order in Incomplete status.
5. Review the terms and select Submit. Purchase orders over $100,000.00 route to the Procurement Operations Manager.

## Notes

- A requisition line can be converted only once.
