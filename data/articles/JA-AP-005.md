# Update supplier remit-to bank details

## Purpose

Use this job aid to change the bank account a supplier is paid to.

## Before you start

- You need the Supplier Bank Maintainer role.
- Every bank change requires a callback to the supplier.

## Steps

1. Go to Payables > Suppliers > Manage Suppliers and open the supplier.
2. On the Payments tab, select the remit-to site and choose Add Bank Account.
3. Call the supplier at the phone number already on file, not the number on the change request, and record the callback date.
4. Enter the new bank details and attach the signed change request form.
5. Select Submit. The change routes to a second Supplier Bank Maintainer for approval.

## Notes

- New bank details stay on a BNK hold for 3 business days after approval.
- Never accept a bank change by email alone.
