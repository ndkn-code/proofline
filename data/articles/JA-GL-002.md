# Reverse a posted journal entry

## Purpose

Use this job aid to reverse a journal that has already posted to the general ledger.

## Steps

1. Go to General Ledger > Journals > Manage Journals.
2. Search for the posted journal by journal name or batch number and open it.
3. Select Reverse and choose the reversal period. The reversal period must be Open.
4. Keep the reversal method Switch Debit and Credit unless the GL Accounting Lead directs otherwise.
5. Select Submit. The reversal journal posts with the prefix REV added to the original name.

## Notes

- You cannot reverse a journal that has already been reversed.
- Reversals of journals over $25,000.00 also route to the GL Accounting Lead for approval.
