# Procedure capture: Void a supplier payment

## Purpose

Use this SOP to void a supplier payment that was issued in error.

## Steps

1. Go to Payables > Payments > Manage Payments.
2. Search for the payment by payment number and open it.
3. Select Void and enter the void date and the void reason.
4. Choose the invoice action Unapply so the invoices reopen for payment.
5. Select Submit. Voids of payments over $50,000.00 require AP Operations Supervisor approval.

## Notes

- You cannot void a payment that has already cleared the bank. Raise a refund request instead.
