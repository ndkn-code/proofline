# Procedure capture: Post an intercompany journal

## Purpose

Use this job aid to record a journal between two Halden Supply Co. entities.

## Steps

1. Go to General Ledger > Journals > Create Journal.
2. Choose the journal category Intercompany.
3. Enter the receiving entity in the Intercompany Partner field on each line.
4. Confirm the due-to and due-from accounts balance for each entity.
5. Select Submit for Approval.

## Notes

- Intercompany journals route to the controllers of both entities.
