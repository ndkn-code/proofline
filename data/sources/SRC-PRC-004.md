# Procedure capture: Receive goods against a purchase order

## Purpose

Use this job aid to record a receipt when goods arrive.

## Steps

1. Go to Purchasing > Receipts > Receive Items.
2. Enter the purchase order number and select Search.
3. Enter the quantity received on each line. Do not receive more than the ordered quantity.
4. Attach the packing slip and select Submit.

## Notes

- Receive goods within 2 business days of delivery so the invoice can match.
- A receipt clears the RCV hold on a matched invoice.
