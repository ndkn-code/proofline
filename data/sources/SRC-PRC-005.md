# Procedure capture: Request a new supplier

## Purpose

Use this SOP to ask Supplier Onboarding to register a new supplier.

## Steps

1. Go to Purchasing > Suppliers > Supplier Registration Request.
2. Enter the supplier legal name, tax classification and contact email.
3. Attach the supplier tax form or the foreign equivalent.
4. Select Submit. Supplier Onboarding reviews the request within 5 business days.

## Notes

- Do not create a purchase order for a supplier until the registration is approved.
