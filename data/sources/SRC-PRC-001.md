# Procedure capture: Create a purchase requisition

## Purpose

Use this SOP to request goods or services through a purchase requisition.

## Steps

1. Go to Purchasing > Requisitions > Create Requisition.
2. Search the catalog, or select Noncatalog Request for items that are not in the catalog.
3. Enter the quantity, the need-by date and the deliver-to location.
4. Enter the charge account. The cost center defaults from your profile.
5. Select Submit. Requisitions over $2,500.00 route to your department approver.

## Notes

- Requisitions for software or IT hardware must use the IT catalog.
- Draft requisitions are deleted after 60 days.
