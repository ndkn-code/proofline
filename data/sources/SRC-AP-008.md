# Procedure capture: Approve a non-PO invoice from the worklist

## Purpose

Use this job aid when an invoice without a purchase order is waiting for your approval.

## Steps

1. Open the approval notification from your worklist.
2. Review the invoice image, the amount and the cost center.
3. Select Approve, Reject or Request Information.
4. Add a comment when you reject or request information.

## Notes

- Non-PO invoices over $10,000.00 also require a second approver from Finance.
