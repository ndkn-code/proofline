# Procedure capture: Resolve a supplier statement discrepancy

## Purpose

Use this SOP to reconcile a supplier statement against open invoices and payments.

## Steps

1. Go to Payables > Suppliers > Supplier Balances and open the supplier.
2. Compare the supplier statement to the open invoices and payments listed.
3. For each missing invoice, request a copy from the supplier before you enter it.
4. Record the reconciliation result in the supplier notes.

## Notes

- Statements older than 90 days can be filed without reconciliation.
