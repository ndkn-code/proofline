# Procedure capture: Apply a credit memo to a supplier balance

## Purpose

Use this SOP to record a supplier credit memo and apply it to an open invoice.

## Steps

1. Go to Payables > Invoices > Create Invoice and set the invoice type to Credit Memo.
2. Enter the credit amount as a negative number.
3. Enter the original invoice in the Related Invoice field.
4. Select Validate, then Save and Close.

## Notes

- Credit memos over $10,000.00 need AP Operations Supervisor review.
