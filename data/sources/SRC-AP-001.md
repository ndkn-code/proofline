# Procedure capture: Enter a supplier invoice manually

## Purpose

Use this job aid to key a supplier invoice that did not arrive through the invoice inbox.

## Before you start

- You need the Payables Clerk role.
- Have the supplier invoice PDF ready to attach.

## Steps

1. Go to Payables > Invoices > Create Invoice.
2. Enter the supplier name, supplier site and invoice number exactly as printed on the invoice.
3. Enter the invoice date, invoice amount and payment terms.
4. For a PO invoice, enter the purchase order number in the Identifying PO field.
5. Attach the invoice PDF and select Validate.
6. Select Save and Close. The invoice enters the approval workflow.

## Notes

- Duplicate invoice numbers for the same supplier are blocked at validation.
- Invoices dated more than 90 days in the past need AP Operations Supervisor review.
