# Procedure capture: Submit a department budget for approval

## Purpose

Use this job aid to submit your department budget version for review.

## Steps

1. Go to Planning > Budgets > My Submissions.
2. Open your department and confirm the budget version Working.
3. Select Run Checks. Fix every error before you continue.
4. Select Submit for Approval. The budget routes to your division finance partner.

## Notes

- After submission the version is read-only until it is approved or returned.
