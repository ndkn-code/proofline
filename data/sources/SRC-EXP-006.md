# Procedure capture: Book travel through the travel portal

## Purpose

Use this job aid to book flights and hotels for business travel.

## Steps

1. Go to Expenses > Travel > Book Travel.
2. Search flights and hotels for your trip dates.
3. Choose economy fares. Hotel rates must be under $220.00 per night.
4. Select Request Approval for trips over $1,500.00.
