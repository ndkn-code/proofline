# Procedure capture: Cancel a requisition

## Purpose

Use this job aid to cancel a requisition you no longer need.

## Steps

1. Go to Purchasing > Requisitions > Manage Requisitions.
2. Open the requisition and select Actions > Cancel Requisition.
3. Enter a cancellation reason and select Submit.

## Notes

- You cannot cancel a requisition line that is already on a purchase order.
