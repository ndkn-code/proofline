# Procedure capture: Close an accounting period (release 25.2)

## Purpose

Use this SOP to close a general ledger accounting period for entities still on release 25.2.

## Before you start

- You need the Period Close Manager role.

## Steps

1. Go to General Ledger > Period Status > Open and Close Periods.
2. Confirm the Payables and Purchasing subledgers are Closed for the period.
3. Run the Unposted Journals report and resolve every item it lists.
4. Select the period and change the status from Open to Closed.
5. Email the completed close checklist to the GL Accounting Lead.

## Notes

- Release 25.2 does not include the Close Checklist page.
- The period close deadline is business day 4 at 17:00 Central.
