# Procedure capture: Set up reimbursement direct deposit

## Purpose

Use this job aid to choose the bank account your expense reimbursements are paid to.

## Steps

1. Go to Expenses > Profile > Payment Methods.
2. Select Add Bank Account and enter your routing number and account number.
3. Select Save. A test deposit posts within 2 business days.
