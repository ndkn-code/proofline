# Procedure capture: Raise a purchase request for goods or services

## Purpose

Use this job aid when you need to buy something and must raise a request first.

## Steps

1. Navigate to Purchasing > Requisitions > Create Requisition.
2. Find the item in the catalog, or choose Noncatalog Request if it is not listed.
3. Fill in quantity, need-by date and deliver-to location.
4. Add the charge account; your cost center fills in from your profile.
5. Submit the request. Requisitions above $2,500.00 go to your department approver.

## Notes

- Use the IT catalog for software or IT hardware.
- Draft requisitions are removed after 60 days.
