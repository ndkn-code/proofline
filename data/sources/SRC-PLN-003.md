# Procedure capture: Run a budget versus actual variance report

## Purpose

Use this job aid to compare actual results with the approved budget.

## Steps

1. Go to Planning > Reports > Variance Analysis.
2. Select the fiscal year, the period range and your department.
3. Choose the comparison Actual vs Budget.
4. Select Run. Variances over 10% are highlighted.
5. Select Export to share the report.

## Notes

- Actuals refresh nightly from the general ledger at 02:00 Central.
